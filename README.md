## US Walmart Sales Using Tableau:

![US Walmart Sales Tableau Dashboard](https://github.com/anandshaw123/US-Walmart-Sales-Tableau-Dashboard-/blob/main/US%20Walmart%20Sales%20By%20Tableau.png)



### Objective:

The main objective of the project shown in this dashboard is to analyze and visualize sales and profit data for Walmart across different states, categories, customers, and time periods. Key goals include:

**Identifying Profitability by State:** Understanding which states contribute the most to profit and which incur losses.

**Customer Insights:** Highlighting top customers based on profitability.

**Category Performance:** Assessing sales performance across various product categories.

**Time Analysis:** Analyzing sales and profit trends over time to identify seasonal patterns or growth opportunities.

**Actionable Insights:** Providing insights to decision-makers to optimize sales strategies, product distribution, and resource allocation.
